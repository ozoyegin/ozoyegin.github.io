Markit for Android

Build a vocabulary habit that fits your day.

From a new list to a completed review session, Markit helps you collect useful language, revisit it deliberately, and understand your progress.

Organise your vocabulary

Create personal lists, see what is due, and set a daily learning target that works for you.

Choose a smart study mode

Learn new records in batches, review records when due, or practise a whole list in game modes.

Strengthen long-term memory

Successful answers advance records through levels, making your growing knowledge visible after every session.

Practise with vocabulary games

Use Match, Guess, Flashcards, and Write to practise in different ways and keep sessions engaging.

Read, mark, and listen

Use the built-in Miray Browser to save language from web pages, keep its context, and listen as you read.

Stay in control of every record

Edit the word or phrase, write your own translation, and keep the original source sentence before saving.

Track detailed insights

Follow your daily target, time studied, due records, completion rate, and progress over the last 7 and 30 days.

Markit Highlighter for Chrome

Save language while you read.

Highlight a useful word or phrase on a web page, keep its context, listen at your own pace, and add it to the list you are studying.

Read and listen in context

Use the floating reader controls to play, pause, repeat, adjust speed, and follow the text as you read.

Select a word or phrase

Select the language you want to remember and add it directly from the page without losing your place.

Add your own translation

Review the selected phrase, write a translation, and save it with the source context for later practice.

Keep lists organised

Choose where each record is saved, browse your lists, and see each list’s learning progress at a glance.

Turn reading into a study habit

See today’s goal, due items, and study time alongside your saved lists—then move from reading to practice whenever you are ready.
